Section II.A
Nature of the Relationship
How supervisor and supervisee roles are defined — A unique aspect of my relationship with both supervisors, as a Senior Applicant, is that each brings decades of experience as a neuropsychologist to the endeavor. As a result, the relationship functions as a hybrid of supervision and consultation, one from which we all benefit through the exchange. The agreements we executed to define our respective responsibilities reflect this structure.

Edward A. Peck III, Ph.D., ABPP-CN

Scope of Supervision
  • General neuropsychology — clinical assessment, diagnostic formulation, and treatment and management recommendations
  • Forensic neuropsychology — forensic evaluation methodology, medicolegal report writing, and testimony-related considerations
  • Neuropsychological consultation — interdisciplinary collaboration and consultation with medical, legal, and other professional referral sources
  • Neuropsychological practice ethics — application of the APA Ethics Code and ABN-related standards, and professional conduct in clinical and forensic contexts
  • Current neuropsychological research — review and application of the empirical literature to clinical decision-making and evidence-based practice
  • The precise focus of supervision will vary, shaped by the cases at hand and the discussion opportunities that arise given the ad hoc nature of this process
Modality
In Person 1.0–1.5 hours per month at his office in Richmond, VA
Zoom 1.0–1.5 hours per month, remote
Format
In Person Present and review a neuropsychological test battery including history, summary scores, raw data, and report; discuss in light of extant and recent literature.
Zoom Present and discuss a current manuscript, chapter, or literature draft that aligns, where possible, with active didactic topics.
