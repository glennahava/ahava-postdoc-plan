Guiding Standard — Handbook p. 8. Each of the following 10 areas will be covered by a minimum of 2 hours of APA-recognized (or equivalent) study. Formats may be remote/interactive, remote/asynchronous, or live.
1Neuroanatomy
2Neurological Disorders
3Psychiatric Disorders
4Neuroimaging
5Neuropathology
6Neuroscience
7Neurochemistry
8Psychometrics
9Brain-Behavior Relationships
10Neuropsychological Assessment
Scope of Didactics

Each of the 10 areas above will be pursued for a minimum of 2 hours, for a combined total of at least 30 hours of didactic learning over the course of the postdoctoral year.

Examples of Possible Didactic Sources
  • ABN Distance Learning
  • National Academy of Neuropsychology — on-demand education
  • National Academy of Neuropsychology — live webinars
  • Grand Rounds at an academic medical center with neurology or neuropsychology
  • CogScreen AE2 Battery — 2-Day Workshop: Introduction, Background & Administration, Norm Selection, Interpretation, and Case Studies (Gary Kay, Ph.D., ABN)
Example of Reference Sources
  • Kolb, B., & Whishaw, I. Q. (2009). Fundamentals of human neuropsychology (6th ed.). Worth Publishers.
  • Lezak, M. D., Howieson, D. B., & Loring, D. W. (2004). Neuropsychological assessment (4th ed.). Oxford University Press.
Didactic hours completed in each of these areas will be logged and documented alongside the conferences attended during the training year, in support of the requirement described under Requirements.
