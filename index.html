This site presents a proposed ad hoc postdoctoral year of supervised training submitted to the American Board of Professional Neuropsychology (ABN) under the Senior Option, in support of board certification (ABN). It outlines the training requirement being satisfied, the supervision arrangement, the areas of specialty‑specific didactic study, planned conference attendance, learning goals, and a proposed timeline for the year.
