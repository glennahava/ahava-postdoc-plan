Section II.A
Nature of the Relationship
How supervisor and supervisee roles are defined — A unique aspect of my relationship with both supervisors, as a Senior Applicant, is that each brings decades of experience as a neuropsychologist to the endeavor. As a result, the relationship functions as a hybrid of supervision and consultation, one from which we all benefit through the exchange. The agreements we executed to define our respective responsibilities reflect this structure.

In order to best meet the goals of the postdoctoral year as described in the Handbook, I determined that it would be advantageous to work with two supervisors rather than one. Dr. Peck and Dr. Waterworth complement one another while bringing distinct areas of neuropsychological expertise to the training experience. Together, this arrangement more closely approximates a more traditional postdoctoral fellowship, in which residents benefit from the guidance of both a primary supervisor and one or more secondary supervisors.

Scope of Supervision
  • General neuropsychology, including clinical assessment, diagnostic formulation, and treatment/management recommendations
  • Aviation neuropsychology, including evaluation methodology, aeromedical report writing and considerations
  • Neuropsychological evaluations of teens and young adults including interdisciplinary collaboration and consultation
  • Neuropsychological practice ethics, including application of the APA Ethics Code, ABN-related ethical standards, and professional conduct
  • Current and extant neuropsychological research, including review and application of the empirical literature to clinical decision-making and evidence-based practice
  • The exact focus of supervision is fluid and will be determined by cases at hand and opportunities for discussion that arise given the ad hoc nature of this process
Modality
In Person Sixteen (16) to 20 hours of in-person supervision conducted over two or more visits on site at The Psychological Testing Center of San Francisco.
Zoom 1.0–1.5 hours per month, remote
Format
In Person Present, discuss, and receive instruction, modeling, and direct observation on the administration, scoring, interpretation, and report incorporation of neuropsychological instruments not currently in Dr. Ahava's repertoire, or measures he has not used in a while, as well as observation of test administration with adolescents, a population he does not currently serve.
Zoom Present and review a neuropsychological test battery including history, summary scores, raw data, and report; discuss in light of extant and recent literature.
